- 600 entries: 16 independent vowel letters, 2 vowel + anusvara/visarga, 576 consonant forms (36 modern consonant letters x 16 forms: virama, inherent a, 12 vowel signs, anusvara, visarga), 6 chillu letters.

- 337 entries share their Manglish with a different letter. The 13 base groups are below; every consonant+vowel form of a group collides too.
